All will be, as you would say, reconciled at some point in your dance through the mind/body/spirit complex that you amuse yourself by distorting in various ways at this time. This distortion is in no case necessary. It is chosen by each of you as an alternative to understanding the complete unity of thought that binds all things. You are not speaking of similar or like entities or things. You are every thing, every being, every emotion, every event, every situation. You are unity. You are infinity. You are love/light, light/love. You are. This is the Law of One.

-Ra -The Law of One
